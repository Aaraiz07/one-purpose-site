Common questions

Does the app work offline?
Partly. The fonts and the app itself are on your device. The Quran text and translations are downloaded the first time you read a surah and then kept on your device, so anything you have opened before reads offline. You can download surahs ahead of time in Settings.
Where does the Arabic text come from?
The Uthmani text has been verified verse by verse against two independent authoritative references — the Tanzil Project and the Quran.com corpus, both derived from the King Fahd Complex Medina Mushaf. All 6,236 verses match both references exactly.
Can I change the translation without changing the app's language?
Yes. The app language and the verse translation are set separately, so you can read an English interface with Urdu verses, or the reverse.
My language is available but the verses are still in English.
Verse translations are not available in every language the interface supports. Hindi, German, Japanese, and Korean currently fall back to English for the verse text. The translation picker marks these clearly.
I lost my streak.
A streak continues as long as the daily goal is completed on consecutive calendar days, in your device's local time zone. Changing your device time zone or clock can affect this. Email us if something looks wrong.
How do I stop the app sending usage statistics?
Analytics is off unless you switch it on. To turn it off, open Settings in the app and disable it. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is and is not collected.
How do I delete my data?
Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device. To request deletion of crash or analytics records, email us — see the Privacy Policy.
